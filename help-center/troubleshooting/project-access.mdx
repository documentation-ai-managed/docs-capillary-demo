---
title: Cannot Access Project
description: Resolve issues when you're unable to open a project.
---

## Symptoms

- Project shows as 'Locked' or 'Unavailable'
- Error: "Access denied" when opening project

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    ````
    Make sure you are listed as a collaborator under Project Settings > Members.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh session">
    ````
    Log out and log back in at https://capillarydemo.com/login.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact owner">
    ````
    Only project owners can grant new users access. Ask the owner to invite you.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
