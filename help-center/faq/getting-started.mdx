## Overview

Creating your first project in Capillary Demo is easy and only takes a few steps.

## Steps

1. Log in at `https://capillarydemo.com/login`
2. Click "New Project" on the dashboard
3. Enter a name for your project and click "Create"
4. You will be redirected to your new project space

## Tip

<Callout kind="info">All new projects are private by default. You can add teammates later under Project Settings.</Callout>
