## Quick Tutorial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your project">
    ````
    Select your project from the dashboard.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Page'">
    ````
    Use the 'Add Page' button in the sidebar navigation.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    ````
    Enter a title and (optionally) a description, then click 'Create'.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start writing">
    ````
    Use the editor to add your content. Changes are saved automatically.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
